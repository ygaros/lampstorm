Lampstorm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short:

  • Your game progress is stored on your device. We don't run any servers and we have no user accounts.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • Google provides the in-game purchases, the optional rewarded ads and the optional Google Play Games features. Google processes data for these as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you choose whether ads may be personalised, and you can change that choice in the game's settings at any time.

1. Who we are

Lampstorm (the "Game") is made by [full business name of the JDG], [business address], NIP [NIP] ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy, except where Google acts as an independent controller (see section 3).

Contact for privacy questions: [contact email]

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, settings, cosmetics, in-game currency, local leaderboard and records of purchases you have made in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in Android settings.

The Game asks for the vibration permission only for haptic feedback. It doesn't ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

3. Services provided by Google

Google Play Billing (in-game purchases)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment card or billing details. For each purchase, Google gives us an order number, a purchase token, the product bought, the date, the price and the country. We use these to deliver the item, to restore items you are entitled to, to handle refunds and to keep the accounting records required by Polish tax law.

Google AdMob (optional rewarded ads)

The Game shows ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward. There are no banner or interstitial ads. To show an ad, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise ads.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the Game's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you use Google Play Games with the Game, Google shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar with the Game. Your scores and achievements are sent to Google so they can appear on leaderboards that other players can see. You control your Play Games profile and its visibility in the Google Play Games app.

Google processes data for these services as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. How long we keep data

  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Purchase records: for as long as Polish tax law requires (normally 5 years from the end of the tax year).
  • Emails you send us: until your question is resolved, then up to 12 months.
  • Data held by Google: according to Google's own retention rules.

6. International transfers

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before. To use these rights, email us at the address in section 1.

Because the Game has no accounts and we don't run servers, most of your data never reaches us. Uninstalling the Game deletes the data stored on your device. For data held by Google, use your Google Account settings.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.

9. Changes to this policy

If the Game starts processing data in a new way, we will update this page and change the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the Game.
